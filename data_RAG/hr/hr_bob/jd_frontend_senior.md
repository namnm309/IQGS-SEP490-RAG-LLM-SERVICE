# Job Description — Senior Frontend Engineer

**Hiring manager:** Bob (HR)  
**Level:** SWE4 equivalent  
**Team:** Customer Experience Web

## Must-have
- 5+ years frontend (React, TypeScript).
- Performance optimization (Core Web Vitals).
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA).
- Design system consumption and contribution.

## Nice-to-have
- Next.js App Router.
- GraphQL with Apollo or similar.
- Micro-frontend experience.

## Interview focus
- Component architecture and state management at scale.
- Rendering performance and bundle strategy.
- Collaboration with design on design tokens.

## Notes
- Remote-friendly; timezone overlap UTC+7 required 4h/day.
