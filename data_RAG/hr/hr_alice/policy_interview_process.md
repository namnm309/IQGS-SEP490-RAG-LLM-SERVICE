# Company Interview Policy — Engineering Roles

## Duration
- Screening: 30 minutes
- Technical: 60 minutes
- System design: 60 minutes
- Behavioral / culture: 45 minutes

## DEI
- Use structured rubric (SWE level guides in system KB).
- Avoid questions about age, family plans, religion, or nationality.
- Provide accommodations when requested in advance.

## Evaluation form
Interviewers must score each competency 1–5 and attach at least one evidence note per score.
