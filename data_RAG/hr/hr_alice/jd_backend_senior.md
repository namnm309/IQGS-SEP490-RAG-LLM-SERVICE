# Job Description — Senior Backend Engineer

**Hiring manager:** Alice (HR)  
**Level:** SWE4 equivalent  
**Team:** Payments Platform

## Must-have
- 5+ years backend development (Java, Go, or Python).
- REST/gRPC APIs, PostgreSQL, Redis.
- Experience with high-throughput payment or fintech systems.
- On-call rotation experience.

## Nice-to-have
- Kafka or similar event streaming.
- PCI-DSS awareness.
- AWS (ECS, RDS, ElastiCache).

## Interview focus for this role
- System design for idempotent payment processing.
- Database transaction patterns and reconciliation.
- Behavioral: ownership in regulated environments.

## Company policy (excerpt)
- 4 interview rounds: screening, technical deep-dive, system design, culture.
- No live coding on candidate laptop; use company sandbox.
- Feedback submitted within 24h using standard evaluation form.
