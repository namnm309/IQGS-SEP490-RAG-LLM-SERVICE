# Interview Rubric — SWE Level 4 (SWE4)

## Level expectation
- 5+ years professional software engineering experience.
- Designs and owns medium-to-large features across services.
- Mentors SWE1–SWE3; drives technical decisions with trade-off analysis.
- Strong in system design, code quality, and cross-team collaboration.

## Technical competencies
1. **System design**: distributed systems, APIs, data modeling, scalability, failure modes.
2. **Coding**: production-quality code, testing, debugging, performance tuning.
3. **Architecture**: service boundaries, observability, security basics, CI/CD awareness.
4. **Domain depth**: backend (APIs, databases, messaging) or frontend (performance, a11y) as per role.

## Behavioral competencies
- Ownership of outcomes and incident follow-up.
- Clear communication with PM, QA, and peers.
- Conflict resolution and constructive feedback.

## Question difficulty guide
| Difficulty | Focus |
|------------|--------|
| Easy | Fundamentals, past project specifics |
| Medium | Design trade-offs, debugging stories |
| Hard | Large-scale design, org-level impact, ambiguity |

## Scoring (1–5)
- 5: Exceeds SWE4 bar with evidence
- 4: Meets bar consistently
- 3: Mixed signals; needs probe
- 2: Below bar for level
- 1: Does not meet minimum
